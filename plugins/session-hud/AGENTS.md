# session-hud

Usage/privacy: [README.md](README.md).

## Module map

- `hooks/register.tsx`: every `on(...)`, atom and `$` call. `session.start` primes the card from `$.session.model()`, `$.session.usage()`, the clock and the theme, and reads the compaction window (`rawMaxTokens` of `$.session.usage({ breakdown: 'summary' })`, a local estimate) again on every `config.set`; `session.measure` replaces the context, the windows and the cost; `turn.start` keeps the cost the main session's turn began at and its start, and beats the clock every second until `turn.complete` (30 s between turns); `turn.step` and `turn.complete` of the main session (no `agentId`) bring the effort, the answering model, the cache share and the turn's duration; `agent.spawn` (after `next`) and the `turn.start`, `turn.step` and `turn.complete` of an `agentId` track the running agents and their models, and `$.agent.list()` drops the ones it shows idle or ended, on the 30 s tick and after a main `turn.complete`, only while the card shows some; a post-result `tool.call` hook counts the main session's edits and errors; `config.set` of `theme` repaints. The `AbovePrompt` render reads the atoms only, with its own running turn (`card.turnStartedAt`) for the dot and yields to a survey.
- `hooks/card.ts`: pure card updates (`measured`, `stepped`, `windowed`, `began`, `completed`, `tooled`; the agents: `spawned`, `agentBegan`, `agentStepped`, `agentEnded`, `reconciled`). `hooks/usage.ts`: pure figures (`paceOf`: the elapsed-time tick, the points ahead of an even pace, the projection at reset and the time to 100%; `cacheShare`, `span`, `money`). `hooks/view.ts`: the card as rows from one snapshot (`cardView`, `modelName`), sized to `bodyColumns`; the two windows sit side by side from 72 inner cells, each bar at most 30 cells with its percent beside it, the second window right after the first block; from `WIDE` inner cells the context and both windows are three aligned blocks (`wideRows`). Turns and output tokens count from the plugin's load; the rate and age come from the host's `startedAt`. `hooks/palette.ts`: a style to its paint, the design's fixed colours under a full-colour dark or automatic theme and theme keys under any other.
- `types/index.d.ts`: the `session-hud` state contract (`card` with its running `agents`, `now`, `palette`).

## Invariants

- Observation never changes what the host answered: every hook passes `next(e)`'s result on as it came, and an observation failure is one debug line. `tests/register.test.ts` (`main-session edits and errors are counted after their result, which goes on unchanged; a subagent counts nothing`).
- Only figures, Claude model names (`modelName`: family and version, anything else `?`), known efforts, step ids and owners reach the screen; never a prompt, an answer, a tool input, a path or a cost line from another source. `tests/view.test.ts` (`only Claude model ids reach the screen, named by family and version`, `a spend limit and malformed figures never reach the screen as numbers`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the card draws the session as measured: model, effort, context, cache, cost, both windows and the turn`).
- A figure the host has not reported is left out, never drawn as zero; the card is empty with nothing to show. `tests/view.test.ts` (`what the host has not reported is left out instead of drawn as zero`).
- The context percent and tokens are measured against the window the host compacts at (`contextWindow`), the model's window only when it is unknown. `tests/view.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the window the host compacts at, and its tokens are shown`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the configured compaction window, read again after a setting changes`).
- A wide card draws the context and both windows as three blocks, with the session rate and age on the first row. `tests/view.test.ts` (`a wide card sets the context and both windows as three blocks, with the session rate and age, turns and output`).
- The dot is yellow with the turn's time only while a main-session turn runs; the one-second clock runs only then. Rendering reads only this plugin's state, and background work does not change the dot. `tests/view.test.ts` (`while a turn runs the dot turns yellow and the turn time counts; between turns it is green with no time`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a running turn shows its time, beating every second until it completes`, `the card reads only its own state and background work does not make its dot busy`).
- The running agents are what the host reported: an id from `agent.spawn` or an agent's turn, its model from the spawn or its last step's answer, gone at its `turn.complete` or once `$.agent.list()` shows it idle or ended; one the list does not name stays, and the list adds none. They lead the bottom row grouped by model name (`?` when unknown), drawn only while one runs, and an agent's turn never touches the main turn, its dot or its cost. `tests/card.test.ts` (`the agent list removes the agents it shows idle or ended, keeps the ones it does not list and adds none`), `tests/view.test.ts` (`the running agents lead the turn row, grouped by model, the most first; none running draws nothing`, `an agent whose model is unknown or not a Claude id is drawn as ?, and a narrow card cuts the row to its width`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a spawned subagent shows with its model until its turn completes, and the spawn answer goes on unchanged`, `a subagent turn neither starts the main turn nor moves its cost baseline`, `the agent list removes a subagent it shows completed, on the clock and after a main turn`).
- The pace: the tick is the share of the window passed; `▲N` is the whole points used above an even pace; the projection appears once 2% of the window has passed, and `100% in` replaces it when the pace reaches 100 before the reset. `tests/usage.test.ts` (`a window half passed at 74% is 24 points ahead and reaches 100% before its reset`, `a window that just began projects nothing past what is used`).

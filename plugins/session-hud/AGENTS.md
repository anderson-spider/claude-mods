# session-hud

Usage/privacy: [README.md](README.md).

## Module map

- `hooks/register.tsx`: every `on(...)`, atom and `$` call. `session.start` primes the card from `$.session.model()`, `$.session.usage()`, the clock and the theme, and reads the compaction window (`rawMaxTokens` of `$.session.usage({ breakdown: 'summary' })`, a local estimate) again on every `config.set`; `session.measure` replaces the context and the windows; `turn.start` keeps the main session's turn start, clears its running context and beats the clock every second until `turn.complete` (30 s between turns); `turn.step` of the main session (no `agentId`) brings the effort, the answering model, the cache share, when the cache was read or written and the running turn's context (first prompt, last prompt plus output), and its `turn.complete` the duration and the turn's place in the history; `classic.PostModelSwitch` (after `next`) keeps the cache lifetime it names, the only event that does, and forgets the cache's last use on a switch to another model; `classic.PostCompact` of the main session (no `agent_id`, after `next`) marks the next turn to join the history; the main step lands before the clock is read (the cache's use is a second update), and `turn.start` clears the running context before reading it, so neither is overtaken; `agent.spawn` (after `next`, unless refused) and the `turn.start` and `turn.complete` of an `agentId` track the running agents, its `turn.step` names a tracked agent's model, and `$.agent.list()` drops the ones it shows idle or ended, on the 30 s tick and after a main `turn.complete`, only while the card shows some; a post-result `tool.call` hook counts the main session's edits and errors; `config.set` of `theme` repaints. The `AbovePrompt` render reads the atoms only, with its own running turn (`card.turnStartedAt`) for the dot and yields to a survey; on the `terminal` surface it draws `cardView`'s rows, on any other `desktopView`'s lines with `Box`, `Text` and `Svg`.
- `hooks/card.ts`: pure card updates (`measured`, `stepped`, `touchedCache`, `cacheUsed`, `switched`, `compactedNow`, `windowed`, `began`, `timed`, `completed` (the last `HISTORY` turns), `tooled`; the agents: `spawned`, `agentBegan`, `agentStepped`, `agentEnded`, `reconciled`). `hooks/usage.ts`: pure figures (`paceOf`: the elapsed-time tick, the points ahead of an even pace, the projection at reset and the time to 100%; `cacheShare`, `span`). `hooks/view.ts`: the card as rows from one snapshot (`cardView`, `modelName`), sized to `bodyColumns`; the two windows sit side by side from 72 inner cells, each bar at most 30 cells with its percent beside it, the second window right after the first block; from `WIDE` inner cells the context and both windows are three aligned blocks (`wideRows`). Turns and output tokens count from the plugin's load. `hooks/desktop.ts`: the card for a laid-out surface as lines of groups of segments and SVG drawings, each group wrapping whole, and spacers (`desktopView`): fixed 96-pixel bars (`barSvg`), the history chart against the compaction window (`historySvg`: grey for what a turn began with, green for what it added, a dashed line on a turn marked `compacted`), the last turn's delta and output (`lastTurnCells`) and the cache line (`cacheLine`: share, age, and the lifetime's rest only when the host named it). `hooks/palette.ts`: a style to its paint, the design's fixed colours under a full-colour dark or automatic theme and theme keys under any other.
- `types/index.d.ts`: the `session-hud` state contract (`card` with its running `agents`, `history` and `pending` turn, `now`, `palette`).

## Invariants

- Observation never changes what the host answered: every hook passes `next(e)`'s result on as it came, and an observation failure is one debug line. `tests/register.test.ts` (`main-session edits and errors are counted after their result, which goes on unchanged; a subagent counts nothing`).
- Only figures, Claude model names (`modelName`: family and version, anything else `?`), known efforts, step ids and owners reach the screen; never a prompt, an answer, a tool input, a path or money: the card has no cost. `tests/view.test.ts` (`only Claude model ids reach the screen, named by family and version`, `a spend limit and malformed figures never reach the screen as numbers`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the card draws the session as measured: model, effort, context, cache, both windows and the turn, and no money`), `tests/desktop.test.ts` (`the desktop card holds the head with the agents, the context with its history, the cache, both windows and the turn, and no money`, `malformed turns and a light theme never reach the drawing as numbers or the dark colours`).
- A figure the host has not reported is left out, never drawn as zero; the card is empty with nothing to show. `tests/view.test.ts` (`what the host has not reported is left out instead of drawn as zero`). The cache lifetime is never assumed: without a model switch the cache line says only how long ago it was used (`tests/desktop.test.ts`: `the cache line says how long ago it was used and, with a lifetime the host named, what is left or that it expired`).
- The context percent and tokens are measured against the window the host compacts at (`contextWindow`), the model's window only when it is unknown. `tests/view.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the window the host compacts at, and its tokens are shown`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the configured compaction window, read again after a setting changes`).
- A wide card draws the context and both windows as three blocks of two lines under the head row; the context's tokens, cache and output share its second line, cut to `CONTEXT_BLOCK` so the windows keep fixed columns. `tests/view.test.ts` (`a wide card sets the context and both windows as three blocks, with turns and output`, `on a wide card the context tokens, cache and output share one line and the windows keep their columns`, `on a wide card without the context tokens, cache and output take their own line under the bar`).
- The dot is yellow with the turn's time only while a main-session turn runs; the one-second clock runs only then. Rendering reads only this plugin's state, and background work does not change the dot. `tests/view.test.ts` (`while a turn runs the dot turns yellow and the turn time counts; between turns it is green with no time`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a running turn shows its time, beating every second until it completes`, `the card reads only its own state and background work does not make its dot busy`).
- The running agents are what the host reported: an id from a spawn the host did not refuse or an agent's turn start, its model from the spawn or its last step's answer (a step adds no agent), gone at its `turn.complete` or once `$.agent.list()` shows it idle or ended; one the list does not name stays, and the list adds none. They lead the bottom row grouped by model name (`?` when unknown), drawn only while one runs, and an agent's turn or step never touches the main turn, its dot, model, effort, cache, output or history. `tests/card.test.ts` (`a spawned agent runs on its model; a turn start adds one unknown, a step names the model that answered and adds none, its end removes it`, `the agent list removes the agents it shows idle or ended, keeps the ones it does not list and adds none`), `tests/view.test.ts` (`the running agents lead the turn row, grouped by model, the most first; none running draws nothing`, `an agent whose model is unknown or not a Claude id is drawn as ?, and a narrow card cuts the row to its width`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a spawned subagent shows with its model until its turn completes, and the spawn answer goes on unchanged`, `a subagent turn does not start the main turn`, `the agent list removes a subagent it shows completed, on the clock and after a main turn`, `a subagent step answered by another model leaves the main session model, effort, cache and output as they were`, `a refused spawn passes its deny unchanged and tracks nothing`).
- The pace: the tick is the share of the window passed; `▲N` is the whole points used above an even pace; the projection appears once 2% of the window has passed, and `100% in` replaces it when the pace reaches 100 before the reset. `tests/usage.test.ts` (`a window half passed at 74% is 24 points ahead and reaches 100% before its reset`, `a window that just began projects nothing past what is used`).
- A turn's history bar is what its own steps reported: the first request's prompt (input, cache read and written), the last prompt plus its output; the last twelve kept, the newest at the right; desktop bars keep a fixed width whatever the card's width. `tests/card.test.ts` (`a turn keeps its first prompt and its last prompt plus output, and joins the history when it ends, the last twelve kept`, `a step that reads or writes the cache marks when; a switch keeps only a 5m or 1h lifetime and forgets the use on another model`, `a compaction marks the next turn to join the history, once, and waits through a turn with no usage`), `tests/desktop.test.ts` (`a bar is a fixed width, lit to the percent and capped at 100, with the tick where the window stands`, `the history stacks what each turn began with under what it added, against the window, and marks a compaction the host reported`, `each window and each part of the context line is a group of its own, so a narrow card wraps between them`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`on the desktop the card is laid out as lines with short drawn bars, the turn history and the cache lifetime a model switch names`, `a compaction the host reports dashes the next turn in the history, and a switch to another model forgets the cache use`, `a subagent compaction leaves the main history unmarked`).

# session-hud

Usage/privacy: [README.md](README.md).

## Module map

- `hooks/register.tsx`: every `on(...)`, atom and `$` call. `session.start` primes the card from `$.session.model()`, `$.session.usage()`, the clock and the theme, and reads the compaction window (`rawMaxTokens` of `$.session.usage({ breakdown: 'summary' })`, a local estimate) again on every `config.set`; `session.measure` replaces the context and the windows; `turn.start` keeps the main session's turn start, clears its running context and beats the clock every second until `turn.complete` (30 s between turns); `turn.step` of the main session (no `agentId`) brings the effort, the answering model, the cache share, when the cache was read or written and the running turn's context (first prompt, last prompt plus output), and its `turn.complete` the duration and the turn's place in the history (the last turn and the one before it); `register`'s `cacheTtl` option (`5m` or `1h`, anything else off) is the lifetime the render draws while the card holds none from the host, never stored in the card, so a changed option (a reload) shows at once and never replaces the host's; `classic.PostModelSwitch` (after `next`) keeps the cache lifetime it names, the only event that does, replacing the option's, and forgets the cache's last use on a switch to another model; `classic.PostCompact` of the main session (no `agent_id`, after `next`) marks the next turn to join the history, so what was sent at its start is not known; the main step lands before the clock is read (the cache's use is a second update), and `turn.start` clears the running context before reading it, so neither is overtaken; `agent.spawn` (after `next`, unless refused) and the `turn.start` and `turn.complete` of an `agentId` track the running agents (the spawn's `subagentType` is the agent's type), its `turn.step` names a tracked agent's model and effort and adds the tokens its request processed to the session's agent total (`agentUsed`, every agent step, tracked or not), and `$.agent.list()` drops the ones it shows idle or ended and names the type of one it lists still running, on the 30 s tick and after a main `turn.complete`, only while the card shows some; a post-result `tool.call` hook counts the main session's edits and errors; `config.set` of `theme` repaints. The `AbovePrompt` render reads the atoms only, with its own running turn (`card.turnStartedAt`) for the dot and yields to a survey; it always asks `next(e)` and draws what comes from beneath (another plugin's band, such as suggestions) under the card, and alone when the card has nothing or fails, so it never hides another plugin (`tests/register.test.ts`: `what draws beneath the band stays under the card`); on every surface it draws `layoutView`'s lines with `Box` (each line a wrapping row, each group kept whole) and `Text`, its drawings as `Svg` on a graphical surface and none on the `terminal`; in a terminal a group too wide for the card shrinks and its text is cut at the edge.
- `hooks/card.ts`: pure card updates (`measured`, `stepped`, `touchedCache`, `cacheUsed`, `switched`, `compactedNow`, `windowed`, `began`, `timed`, `completed` (the last `HISTORY` turns), `tooled`; the agents: `spawned`, `agentBegan`, `agentStepped`, `agentEnded`, `reconciled`, `agentUsed`). `hooks/usage.ts`: pure figures (`paceOf`: the elapsed-time tick, the points ahead of an even pace, the projection at reset and the time to 100%; `cacheShare`, `span`). `hooks/view.ts`: the text pieces every surface shares (`modelName`, `typeName`, `tokens`, `contextOf`, `headCells`, `agentCells`, `agentTokenCells`, `cacheCells`, `turnCells`, `shownLimits`). Turns count from the plugin's load. `hooks/layout.ts`: the card as lines of groups of segments and drawings, each group wrapping whole, and spacers (`layoutView`), one layout for every surface; `draw` picks the bars: `svg` (fixed 96-pixel bars, `barSvg`) or `cells` for the terminal (fixed 12-cell text bars, `barCells`). The context line ends with the last turn in figures (`lastTurnCells`: what was sent at its start, `sentOf`, known only from the turn before it and not after a compaction; what it added; its output) and the cache line (`cacheLine`: share, age, and the lifetime's rest only when the host named it). `hooks/palette.ts`: a style to its paint, the design's fixed colours under a full-colour dark or automatic theme and theme keys under any other.
- `types/index.d.ts`: the `session-hud` state contract (`card` with its running `agents`, their `agentTokens`, `history` and `pending` turn, `now`, `palette`).

## Invariants

- Observation never changes what the host answered: every hook passes `next(e)`'s result on as it came, and an observation failure is one debug line. `tests/register.test.ts` (`main-session edits and errors are counted after their result, which goes on unchanged; a subagent counts nothing`).
- Only figures, Claude model names (`modelName`: family and version, anything else `?`), agent type names (`typeName`: a name, anything else `?`), known efforts, step ids and owners reach the screen; never a prompt, an answer, an agent's task description, a tool input, a path or money: the card has no cost. `tests/view.test.ts` (`only Claude model ids reach the screen, named by family and version`, `a spend limit and malformed figures never reach the screen as numbers`, `an agent whose model or type is unknown or not a name is drawn as ?, and an unknown effort is left out`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the card draws the session as measured: model, effort, context, cache, both windows and the turn, and no money; the terminal draws no drawing and cuts what does not fit`), `tests/layout.test.ts` (`the card holds the head with the agents, the context with the last turn, the cache, both windows and the turn, and no money`, `malformed turns and a light theme never reach the drawing as numbers or the dark colours`).
- A figure the host has not reported is left out, never drawn as zero; the card is empty with nothing to show. `tests/view.test.ts` (`what the host has not reported is left out instead of drawn as zero`). The cache lifetime is never guessed: it comes from a model switch or from the person's `cacheTtl` option; without either the cache line says only how long ago it was used (`tests/layout.test.ts`: `the cache line says how long ago it was used and, with a lifetime the host named, what is left or that it expired`; `tests/register.test.ts`: `without cacheTtl the lifetime waits for a model switch`, `with cacheTtl the card shows what is left of the cache from the first turn`, `a model switch's lifetime replaces the cacheTtl option's`).
- The context percent and tokens are measured against the window the host compacts at (`contextWindow`), the model's window only when it is unknown. `tests/view.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the window the host compacts at, and its tokens are shown`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the context is measured against the configured compaction window, read again after a setting changes`). From `CONTEXT_ALERT` (85%) of that window the context bar and its percent take the alert colour (`danger`; the SVG alt adds `near compaction`) on every surface: `tests/layout.test.ts` (`from 85% of the compaction window the context bar and percent turn to the alert colour on every surface`).
- The terminal and the other surfaces draw the same lines and groups; only the drawings differ, and the terminal never gets an `Svg`. `tests/layout.test.ts` (`the terminal card holds the same lines as the desktop, with text bars and no drawing`, `each window and each part of the context line is a group of its own, so a narrow card wraps between them`).
- The dot is yellow with the turn's time only while a main-session turn runs; the one-second clock runs only then. Rendering reads only this plugin's state, and background work does not change the dot. `tests/view.test.ts` (`while a turn runs the dot turns yellow and the turn time counts; between turns it is green with no time`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a running turn shows its time, beating every second until it completes`, `the card reads only its own state and background work does not make its dot busy`).
- The running agents are what the host reported: an id from a spawn the host did not refuse or an agent's turn start, its model from the spawn or its last step's answer, its effort from its last step and its type from the spawn or the agent list (a step adds no agent), gone at its `turn.complete` or once `$.agent.list()` shows it idle or ended; one the list does not name stays, and the list adds none. They sit at the right of the head line grouped by type, model name and effort (a model `?` when unknown, a type `?` when not a name, a part not reported left out), drawn only while one runs, and an agent's turn or step never touches the main turn, its dot, model, effort, cache, output or history. `tests/card.test.ts` (`a spawned agent runs on its model; a turn start adds one unknown, a step names the model that answered and adds none, its end removes it`, `the agent list removes the agents it shows idle or ended, keeps the ones it does not list and adds none`, `a spawn names the type, a step the model and effort; a part that is not a name keeps what was known`, `the agent list names the type of a running agent without one, never replaces a known type, and leaves the card as it was when it has nothing to add`), `tests/view.test.ts` (`the running agents are grouped by type, model and effort, the most first; none running draws nothing`, `an agent whose model or type is unknown or not a name is drawn as ?, and an unknown effort is left out`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`a spawned subagent shows with its type, model and effort until its turn completes, and the spawn answer goes on unchanged`, `a subagent turn does not start the main turn`, `the agent list removes a subagent it shows completed, on the clock and after a main turn, and names the type it lists`, `a subagent step answered by another model leaves the main session model, effort, cache and output as they were`, `a refused spawn passes its deny unchanged and tracks nothing`).
- The agents' total is the sum, over every subagent and teammate step since the plugin loaded, of its request's input, cache read and written, and output tokens; usage with a part that is not a count adds nothing, and the total is left out until one adds. It sits after the running agents on the head line and stays after they end; main-session steps never add to it. `tests/card.test.ts` (`every agent step adds what its request processed to the agents total, and usage that is not a count adds nothing`), `tests/view.test.ts` (`what the agents used is a token total, left out before any usage and never drawn from a malformed figure`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`the tokens every agent step processed add up on the head line, after the agent ends, and main-session steps add none`).
- The pace: the tick is the share of the window passed; `▲N` is the whole points used above an even pace; the projection appears once 2% of the window has passed, and `100% in` replaces it when the pace reaches 100 before the reset. `tests/usage.test.ts` (`a window half passed at 74% is 24 points ahead and reaches 100% before its reset`, `a window that just began projects nothing past what is used`).
- A turn's figures are what its own steps reported: the first request's prompt (input, cache read and written), the last prompt plus its output; the last two kept. What was sent at a turn's start is its first prompt less where the turn before ended, left out without that turn, after a compaction the host reported or when the start is below it; bars keep a fixed width whatever the card's width. `tests/card.test.ts` (`a turn keeps its first prompt and its last prompt plus output, and joins the history when it ends, the last two kept`, `a step that reads or writes the cache marks when; a switch keeps only a 5m or 1h lifetime and forgets the use on another model`, `a compaction marks the next turn to join the history, once, and waits through a turn with no usage`), `tests/layout.test.ts` (`a bar is a fixed width, lit to the percent and capped at 100, with the tick where the window stands`, `a terminal bar is a fixed width, lit to the percent and capped at 100, with the tick where the window stands`, `the last turn says what was sent at its start, what it added and its output; what was sent is left out when it is not known`), `tests/register.test.ts` (`on the desktop the card is laid out as lines with short drawn bars, the last turn and the cache lifetime a model switch names`, `what was sent at a turn's start shows once the turn before it is known, and a switch to another model forgets the cache use`, `after a compaction the host reports, what was sent at the next turn's start is not known`, `a subagent compaction leaves the main turns unmarked`).
